Privacy policy

How Alex Ore handles information when you read, comment, subscribe, or get in touch.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy describes the personal information processed through the alexore website and its Studio dashboard. Alex Ore, based in Lagos, Nigeria, determines why and how this information is processed. To ask a question or exercise a privacy right, use the contact form and write “Privacy request” in your message.

Information we collect and why

  • Comments: If you comment without choosing anonymity, we collect the name you provide, your email address, and the comment. We use these details to review and manage the comment and help prevent abuse. An approved display name and comment are public; your email is not. Anonymous comments do not require a name or email. The lawful basis we rely on for non-anonymous comment details is our legitimate interest in moderating the conversation and preventing abuse, balanced against your rights. We do not use a comment email for newsletters unless you separately select the optional newsletter box.
  • Newsletter: If you join the email list, we store your email address on the basis of your consent so we can manage the subscription and send occasional notes when an email service is connected. Joining is a separate choice. You can withdraw that choice and ask us to remove your address through the contact form.
  • Contact messages: We collect your name, email, subject, and message so we can read and respond to your enquiry, based on our legitimate interest in responding to a request you sent us.
  • Article likes: The site creates a random browser identifier in local storage and sends it to the server, which stores a one-way hash to remember whether that browser has liked an article. It is used to prevent duplicate likes, based on our legitimate interest in operating this feature, not to identify you by name.
  • Technical data: The server and hosting provider may process connection and security information, such as IP address, request time, and browser details, in their ordinary logs for security and service operation. The site does not currently use analytics or automated decisions about visitors.

How information is used and shared

Information is stored in the site database and is accessible to the site administrator for the purposes above. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process it to operate and secure the site. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or needed to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim. Approved comments are the only comment details shown publicly; email addresses are not displayed.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive technical connection information. Links to external sites, media, and checkout services are governed by those providers’ own privacy policies.

Retention

We keep each record only while its stated purpose remains relevant. Comments and associated moderation details remain while needed to moderate or display the comment. Newsletter addresses remain until you withdraw consent or ask us to delete them. Contact messages remain while needed to respond to and document the enquiry. The site does not currently auto-expire database records, so records are removed by an administrator or after a verified deletion request; server logs and backups may follow the hosting provider’s retention schedule. To request removal, contact us using the form linked above.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access to or correction, restriction, objection to, or deletion of your personal information, and may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Send requests through the contact form. We may need to verify your request before acting.

You can choose anonymous commenting. The newsletter checkbox on the comment form is optional and separate from posting a comment. The site uses a PHP session cookie for API rate limiting and Studio sign-in, and local storage for the random article-like identifier; it does not currently run advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting providers may process or store data in locations outside Nigeria, depending on the hosting configuration; contact us for current provider details.

Security and changes

We use access controls and reasonable technical measures to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as the site changes. The latest version and update date will appear on this page.
